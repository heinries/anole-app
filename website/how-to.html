ILLUSTRATED CONCEPT WALKTHROUGH

From a question
to a shared picture.

Anole imagines a simple way to build a community topic. Project creation, publishing, collection and moderation are not implemented.

◎
STEP 01

Choose a community topic

Start with a specific question, such as where neighbours have observed seating and shade.

↓
☷
STEP 02

Choose useful fields

Ask for a place, date and observable details. Wildlife needs a reported species; accessibility needs features and barriers.

↓
▦
STEP 03

Share the project’s QR code

A future organiser could share a public project link. The example QR codes work today; they are not access controls.

↓
▧
STEP 04

Collect observations

Neighbours could add a photo and describe what they saw. Today, Capture only previews a note locally.

↓
⌖
STEP 05

Review the shared map

A future review process could check sources and follow up. Today’s map filters fictional, unverified samples.
